📌 Loss (raw)

Raw step loss is noisy by design. Sharp spikes often coincide with difficult negatives and can be healthy if rolling averages remain controlled.

📌 Rolling-10 / Rolling-50

Rolling-10 catches near-term drift, while Rolling-50 shows structural trend. Divergence between them signals transient instability vs true regression.

📌 Spike & Checkpoint Discipline

Spike count helps spot turbulence regimes. Checkpoints every fixed interval ensure rapid rollback/restart when cluster or process failures hit mid-run.
