1. dataset_strategy
Your existing triad is potent, but each dataset must be strictly compartmentalized by the exact spatial reasoning skill it teaches the Volumetric 3D-RoPE embeddings.
* ViDoRe (The Spatial Anchor):
   * Role: Used strictly to initialize the 128-D ColPali projection head. It teaches the base model the standard MaxSim vector space mapping for 2D bounding boxes and basic layouts.
* DocVQA (The Micro-Detail Extractor):
   * Role: Must be reformatted from QA to a [Query -> Target Image Patch] retrieval task. Forces the local sliding-window attention of Gemma 3 to achieve pixel-perfect localization (e.g., matching a query to an 8pt font footnote) so our spatial pooling doesn't blur out critical details.
* Internal CGI Corpus (The Z-Axis Heavyweight):
   * Role: Annual reports naturally span multiple pages (e.g., narrative on Page 4 referencing a ledger on Page 6). We will chunk these into continuous 8-to-16 page tensors to train the volumetric depth (the Z-axis) of the global attention layers.
Are these enough to win alone? ABSOLUTELY NOT. If you stop here, GemmPali will catastrophically overfit to enterprise financial boilerplate (two-column text, standard serif fonts, SEC-style tables). 3. data_mixing_and_curriculum
We will employ a strictly gated 3-Phase Curriculum to prevent vector space explosion.
Phase 1: 2D Manifold Warmup (20% of Training)
* Ratio: 70% ViDoRe | 30% DocVQA
* Context Length: Strictly 1 to 2 pages per sample.
* Goal: Stabilize the orthogonal initialization of the new ColPali projection head. Freeze the LLM backbone entirely.
Phase 2: Volumetric Z-Axis Expansion (40% of Training)
* Ratio: 40% MP-DocVQA | 30% CGI Corpus | 20% DUDE | 10% ViDoRe
* Context Length: 4 to 8 pages continuous chunks.
* Goal: Unfreeze the top 6 global attention layers of Gemma 3. The global attention layers learn to pass KV states across <page_break> tokens.
Phase 3: The Contrastive Crucible (40% of Training)
* Ratio: 60% CGI Corpus (Strictly Hard Negatives) | 40% MP-DocVQA (Strictly Hard Negatives)
* Context Length: 8 to 16 pages continuous chunks.
* Hard-Negative Strategy (How we beat ColQwen):
   1. Intra-Document Negatives: Query targets Page 5. The batch MUST contain Page 4 and Page 6 of the exact same document.
   2. Temporal Negatives: Pair the 2024 CGI Revenue chart against the 2023 CGI Revenue chart. Layouts are 100% identical; only the numbers differ. This forces mathematically absolute token grounding.

5. multi_page_eval_protocol_vs_colqwen
Standard benchmarks are useless for proving multi-page SOTA. We will build The CGI Cross-Page Crucible.
Apples-to-Apples Setup: Extract 500 contiguous 12-page chunks from the CGI holdout set. Index identically using GemmPali and ColQwen2.5-VL. Apply Int8 Binary Quantization (BQ) in Vespa for both indexes to normalize storage effects.
Benchmark Design (Cross-Page Continuity Queries):
* Table Split Query: "What is the total YoY margin growth for Q4?" (Requires reading headers on Page 6, data row on Page 7).
* Narrative-to-Figure Query: "Based on the risk factors outlined on Page 2, what is the projected deficit in Figure 4?"
Metrics Matrix:
1. Multi-Page nDCG@5 & MRR@10: Standard quality ranking strictly on multi-page continuous documents.
2. Cross-Page Consistency Rate (CPCR): A binary strict metric. A score of 1.0 is awarded only if the model's top retrieved multi-vector spans encapsulate both pages required to answer the query, rather than just the page with the highest textual BM25 overlap.
3. Index Storage Efficiency: Track Megabytes per 100 pages. GemmPali’s 4x sequence pooling must mathematically prove a ~75% storage reduction vs ColQwen's unpooled approach.
Failure Taxonomy & Remediation:
* Z-Axis Depth Collapse: Retrieves the correct table layout, but from the wrong page/year. (Remediation: Increase Phase 3 Temporal Negative sampling weight).
* X/Y-Axis Smearing: Retrieves the correct page, but the bounding box activation bleeds into adjacent paragraphs. (Remediation: Reduce pool_factor from 4 to 2, trading VRAM for higher spatial resolution).
